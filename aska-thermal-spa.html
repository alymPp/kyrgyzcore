      Aska Thermal Spa:
      Hot Pools Under Red Cliffs

      Updated 2026 · 4 min read

      Aska Thermal Spa is a rare kind of place: three open-air pools right under a rocky cliff with red granite. Hot water comes through pipes from a well, while cool water flows naturally from the rock (mineralization: 233 mg/L). Minimal human interference — maximum atmosphere.

      01

      What makes it special?

      The pools sit on the left bank of the river, directly below a dramatic cliff. The contrast is the whole vibe: hot thermal water + cold natural flow + red rocks + fresh air.

      Quick tips before you go

      • 🩳
        Bring swimwear + towel Sounds obvious, but people forget it all the time.
      • 🩴
        Use slippers Wet surfaces + cold air = easy to slip.
      • 🌡️
        Don’t overstay Thermal water is powerful — keep sessions short and comfortable.
      • 📸
        Film the steam Morning or cold weather makes the best “spa in the wild” shots.
