      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is the best time to visit Kyrgyzstan?
      May to October is best for this route: stable weather, accessible mountain areas and comfortable temperatures.
      Do I need a visa for Kyrgyzstan?
      Many nationalities enter visa-free, but rules depend on your passport. Check current requirements before booking.
      What fitness level is required?
      Moderate. Walks and easy hikes are adjustable. Activities like horses are optional.
